Rainbow Chicken Salad with Almond Honey Mustard Dressing

A colorful chicken salad drizzled with sweet almond honey‑mustard dressing.

Directions
Arrange the salad

Place the greens in a bowl and arrange the sliced chicken, cabbage, carrots, tomatoes, and almonds on top.

Make the dressing

Whisk the almond butter, honey, mustard, water, and lemon juice until smooth.

Toss and serve

Drizzle the dressing over the salad and toss gently before serving.
