Creamy Vegan Mushroom Risotto

A silky risotto made with mushrooms, garlic, and vegetable broth.

For the risotto
Directions
Sauté mushrooms

Heat the olive oil and sauté the mushrooms until golden, then set aside.

Start the risotto

Sauté the onion and garlic, then add the rice and toast for 1 minute.

Add broth gradually

Add the warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring until absorbed before adding more.

Finish

Stir in the mushrooms, nutritional yeast, and salt. Serve creamy and hot.
