Cauliflower Walnut Vegetarian Taco Meat

A hearty plant‑based taco filling made from roasted cauliflower and walnuts.

Maya Chen

10 January 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time10minutes
vegan

Nutrition Information

Calories 218.4 kcal
Total Fat 14.6 g
Protein 7.2 g
Carbohydrate 17.8 g
Cholesterol 0 mg

Walnuts add healthy fats and a meaty texture; great in tacos or burrito bowls.

Ingredients

For the taco meat
Directions
Preheat the oven

Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking tray.

Pulse the ingredients

Pulse the cauliflower and walnuts in a food processor until they resemble coarse crumbs.

Season and roast

Toss the mixture with olive oil, paprika, cumin, chili powder, salt, and soy sauce, then spread on the tray and roast for 10 minutes.

Serve

Use as a taco filling with tortillas, salsa, and avocado.
