Big and Juicy Wagyu Beef Cheeseburger

A rich, tender Wagyu burger stacked with melted cheese and fresh toppings.

Directions
Form the patties

Divide the Wagyu beef into two equal patties and season both sides with the salt and pepper.

Make the sauce

Mix the mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, and chopped pickles in a small bowl.

Toast the buns

Butter the brioche buns with some of the listed butter and toast them cut-side down in a pan until golden.

Cook the patties

Cook the patties on a hot grill or pan for 3–4 minutes per side, adding the cheddar in the last minute to melt. Cook to a safe internal temperature appropriate for ground beef.

Assemble

Spread the sauce on the buns, add the lettuce, tomato, red onion, and cheesy patties, then close the burgers.

Serve

Serve immediately while hot and juicy.
