Warm Peach Crumble

Sweet peaches baked under a crispy crumble topping.

Henry Dalton

18 March 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time30minutes
dessert

Nutrition Information

Calories 274.9 kcal
Total Fat 10.1 g
Protein 3.8 g
Carbohydrate 43.6 g
Cholesterol 22.0 mg

Best served warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream or a dollop of cream.

Ingredients

For the peach filling For the crumble topping
Directions
Preheat the oven

Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).

Prepare the filling

Toss the sliced peaches with sugar, lemon juice, and cinnamon and spread in a baking dish.

Make the crumble

Rub the flour, oats, brown sugar, salt, and cold butter together until crumbly.

Assemble and bake

Sprinkle the crumble over the peaches and bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Serve

Let cool slightly and serve warm.
