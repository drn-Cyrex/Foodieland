Cheesy Scrambled Eggs

Soft scrambled eggs melted together with rich cheddar cheese.

Daniel Harris

5 April 2024
prep time3minutes
cook time5minutes
breakfast

Nutrition Information

Calories 278.5 kcal
Total Fat 20.4 g
Protein 17.9 g
Carbohydrate 2.1 g
Cholesterol 395.0 mg

Rich and comforting; best enjoyed as an occasional indulgent breakfast.

Ingredients

For main dish
Directions
Whisk the eggs

Beat the eggs with milk, salt, and black pepper until frothy.

Melt the butter

Melt the butter in a non-stick pan over low heat.

Scramble gently

Pour in the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula, cooking until soft curds form.

Add cheese

Stir in the grated cheddar and remove from heat while still slightly creamy.

Serve

Transfer to a plate and serve immediately.
