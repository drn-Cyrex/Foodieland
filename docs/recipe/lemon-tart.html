Fresh Lemon Tart

A bright and tangy lemon tart with a buttery crust.

Clara Whitfield

5 February 2024
prep time30minutes
cook time33minutes
dessert

Tangy and buttery; a little goes a long way thanks to the intense lemon flavor.

Ingredients

For the crust For the lemon filling
Directions
Make the crust

Blend the flour, butter, and powdered sugar until crumbly, then add the egg yolk and form a dough. Chill for 30 minutes.

Blind bake

Roll the dough into a tart pan, line with parchment and weights, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12 minutes. Remove the weights and bake 5 more minutes.

Whisk the filling

Whisk the eggs and sugar, then add the lemon juice, zest, and cream.

Bake the tart

Pour the filling into the crust and bake for 15–18 minutes until just set. Cool completely.

Serve

Slice and serve at room temperature or chilled.
