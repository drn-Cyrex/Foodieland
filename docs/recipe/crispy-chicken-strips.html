Crispy Chicken Strips

Golden chicken strips coated in seasoned breadcrumbs and fried until crisp.

Directions
Set up the breading station

Place the flour in one bowl, the beaten eggs in another, and the breadcrumbs mixed with paprika, salt, and pepper in a third.

Coat the chicken

Dredge each chicken strip in flour, dip it in egg, then coat it with the seasoned breadcrumbs.

Fry

Heat the oil to 350°F (175°C). Fry the strips in batches for 5–7 minutes per batch, turning as needed, until golden and the thickest pieces reach 165°F (74°C).

Drain and serve

Drain on paper towels and serve hot.
