Raspberry Gelato

Smooth Italian-style gelato made with fresh raspberries.

Directions
Blend the raspberries

Purée the raspberries with sugar and lemon juice, then strain to remove seeds.

Combine with dairy

Stir the milk and cream into the raspberry purée until smooth.

Chill

Refrigerate the mixture for at least 2 hours until very cold.

Churn

Churn in an ice cream maker until thick and creamy.

Serve

Transfer to a container and freeze until firm, then scoop and serve.
