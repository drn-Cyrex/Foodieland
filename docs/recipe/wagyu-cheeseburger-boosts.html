Wagyu Cheeseburger with Caramelized Onions and Truffle Mayo

A deluxe Wagyu cheeseburger topped with Swiss cheese, crispy bacon, caramelized onions, and truffle mayonnaise.

Directions
Caramelize the onions

Melt the butter in a pan over low heat. Add the sliced onion and cook for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and deep golden.

Make the truffle mayo

Whisk the mayonnaise, truffle oil, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt.

Cook the patties

Divide the Wagyu into two equal patties, season with the salt and pepper, and cook for 3–4 minutes per side, topping with Swiss cheese during the last minute. Cook to a safe internal temperature appropriate for ground beef.

Cook the bacon and toast the buns

Cook the bacon until crisp, then drain. Toast the brioche buns cut-side down until golden.

Assemble

Spread truffle mayo on the buns, then layer the lettuce, tomato, cheesy patty, bacon, and caramelized onions.

Serve

Serve hot with any remaining truffle mayo on the side.
