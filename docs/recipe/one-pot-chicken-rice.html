Easy One-Pot Chicken and Rice

Seasoned chicken thighs simmered with rice and broth for a comforting one-pot dinner.

Fiona Murray

20 March 2024

cook time25minutes

Minimal cleanup — everything cooks in one pot for an easy weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

For the chicken and rice
Directions
Brown the chicken

Season the chicken thighs with paprika, salt, and pepper, then brown them in olive oil in a large lidded pot.

Sauté aromatics

Remove the chicken and sauté the onion and garlic in the same pot until softened.

Add rice and broth

Stir in the rice, pour in the broth, and return the chicken to the pot.

Simmer

Cover and simmer gently for about 20–25 minutes, until the rice is tender and the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C). Add a little broth or water if the pot becomes too dry before the rice is done.

Rest and serve

Let stand covered for 5 minutes, fluff the rice, sprinkle with parsley, and serve.
