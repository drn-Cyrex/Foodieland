Chicken Meatballs with Cream Cheese

Tender chicken meatballs with a creamy cheese center and a fresh parsley garnish.

Paulina Vega

20 February 2024
prep time15minutes
cook time10minutes
meat

The cream cheese center keeps these meatballs moist and flavorful.

Ingredients

For the meatballs For the cream cheese filling For the garnish
Directions
Mix the meatball base

Combine the ground chicken, breadcrumbs, egg, Parmesan, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until evenly mixed.

Form and fill

Flatten a portion of the mixture, place a cube of cream cheese in the center, and seal the mixture around it to form a ball.

Cook

Brown the meatballs in a skillet over medium heat for 8–10 minutes, turning regularly, until cooked through to an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).

Serve

Sprinkle with the chopped parsley and serve warm.
