Chicken Ramen with Mushrooms

A warm ramen bowl with tender chicken, mushrooms, and a savory ginger-soy broth.

For the ramen
Directions
Cook the chicken

Bring the chicken broth to a gentle simmer with the ginger and soy sauce. Add the thinly sliced chicken and simmer for about 8 minutes, until nearly cooked through.

Add mushrooms

Add the sliced mushrooms and simmer for 5 more minutes.

Cook the noodles

Add the ramen noodles and cook according to the package instructions, until tender and the chicken is fully cooked.

Finish and serve

Stir in the sesame oil, top with green onions, and serve hot.
