Berry Cheesecake Squares

Creamy cheesecake bites layered with fresh berry topping.

Nathan Brooks

22 January 2024
prep time20minutes
cook time25minutes
dessert

Rich and creamy; the fresh berry topping adds a bright contrast.

Ingredients

For the crust For the filling For the berry topping
Directions
Preheat and prepare the crust

Preheat the oven to 325°F (165°C). Mix the graham crumbs, melted butter, and sugar, then press into the bottom of a lined baking pan.

Make the filling

Beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth, then add the eggs and vanilla and mix until just combined.

Bake

Pour the filling over the crust and bake for 20–25 minutes until set. Cool completely, then chill.

Prepare the berry topping

Simmer the berries with sugar and lemon juice for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened. Cool.

Assemble and serve

Spread the berry topping over the chilled cheesecake, cut into squares, and serve.
