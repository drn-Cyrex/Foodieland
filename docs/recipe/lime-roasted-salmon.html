Lime-Roasted Salmon with Ginger Sauce

Salmon roasted with fresh lime and served with a savory-sweet ginger, soy, and sesame sauce.

Hannah Lindqvist

6 February 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time15minutes
meat

Nutrition Information

Calories 328.6 kcal
Total Fat 19.4 g
Protein 32.8 g
Carbohydrate 6.2 g
Cholesterol 84.0 mg

High in omega-3 fatty acids; the lime and ginger keep it fresh and light.

Ingredients

For the salmon For the ginger sauce
Directions
Preheat the oven

Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking tray with parchment.

Season the salmon

Rub the salmon with olive oil, lime zest, salt, and pepper, then place it on the tray.

Roast

Roast for 12–15 minutes, depending on thickness, until the salmon is opaque and flakes easily.

Make the ginger sauce

Whisk the ginger, soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, and lime juice together.

Serve

Drizzle the ginger sauce over the roasted salmon and serve with lime wedges if available.
