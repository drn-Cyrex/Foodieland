Fruity Pancake with Orange & Blueberry

A bright, citrusy pancake bursting with orange zest and juicy blueberries.

cook time8minutes

For main dish For the topping
Directions
Mix the dry ingredients

Whisk together the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a large bowl.

Add wet ingredients

Beat in the egg, milk, and orange zest until the batter is smooth and free of lumps.

Fold in blueberries

Gently fold the fresh blueberries into the batter, being careful not to crush them.

Cook the pancakes

Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat. Pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake and cook until bubbles appear, then flip and cook until golden.

Make the orange-blueberry topping

Simmer the blueberries, orange juice, and maple syrup in a small saucepan for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened.

Serve

Stack the pancakes and spoon the warm orange-blueberry topping over the top. Serve immediately.
