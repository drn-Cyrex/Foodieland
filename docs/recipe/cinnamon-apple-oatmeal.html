Cinnamon Apple Oatmeal

Warm oatmeal with sweet apples and cinnamon spice.

Ethan Cooper

1 June 2024
prep time5minutes
cook time7minutes
breakfast

Nutrition Information

Calories 236.7 kcal
Total Fat 4.5 g
Protein 6.2 g
Carbohydrate 44.8 g
Cholesterol 0 mg

Warm and filling; cinnamon helps balance the sweetness of the apple.

Ingredients

For main dish
Directions
Combine oats and milk

In a saucepan, combine the rolled oats, milk, and a pinch of salt.

Cook the oatmeal

Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until creamy.

Add apple and cinnamon

Stir in the diced apple and ground cinnamon and cook for another 2 minutes until the apple softens.

Sweeten and serve

Drizzle with honey, stir, and serve warm.
