Mediterranean Quinoa Salad

A protein-rich quinoa salad with olives, tomatoes, and herbs.

Yasmin Haddad

1 May 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time2minutes
lunch

Nutrition Information

Calories 276.3 kcal
Total Fat 12.8 g
Protein 8.6 g
Carbohydrate 32.4 g
Cholesterol 0 mg

A meal-prep favorite that keeps well in the fridge for several days.

Ingredients

For the salad For the dressing
Directions
Combine the salad ingredients

Place the quinoa, tomatoes, olives, cucumber, onion, and parsley in a bowl.

Make the dressing

Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, and salt together.

Toss and serve

Pour the dressing over the salad, toss well, and serve chilled or at room temperature.
