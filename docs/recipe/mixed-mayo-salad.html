Mixed Salad with Creamy Mayonnaise Dressing

Crisp lettuce and fresh vegetables tossed in a creamy mayonnaise, yogurt, and mustard dressing.

Natalie Brooks

11 January 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time2minutes
lunch

Nutrition Information

Calories 186.5 kcal

Protein 3.4 g
Carbohydrate 12.7 g
Cholesterol 14.0 mg

A light creamy dressing keeps this crunchy salad fresh and satisfying.

Ingredients

For the salad For the dressing
Directions
Prepare the vegetables

Wash and chop the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, and onion, then place them in a large bowl with the corn.

Make the dressing

Whisk the mayonnaise, yogurt, lemon juice, mustard, salt, and pepper until smooth.

Toss the salad

Drizzle the dressing over the vegetables and toss gently to coat evenly.

Serve

Serve immediately for the best crunch.
