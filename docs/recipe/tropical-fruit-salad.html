Tropical Fruit Salad with Superfood Toppings

A refreshing mix of pineapple, mango, kiwi, and papaya finished with seeds, goji berries, and lime.

24 January 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time2minutes
lunch

Packed with vitamin C and antioxidants thanks to the superfood toppings.

Ingredients

For the fruit mix For the superfood boost
Directions
Prepare the fruit

Chop all the tropical fruit into bite-sized pieces and place in a large bowl.

Add citrus

Drizzle the lime juice over the fruit and toss gently.

Add superfoods

Sprinkle the chia seeds, goji berries, and hemp seeds over the top.

Sweeten and serve

Drizzle with maple syrup and serve fresh.
