Classic Caesar Salad

Crisp romaine lettuce with creamy Caesar dressing and crunchy croutons.

Marco Bellini

6 March 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time2minutes
lunch

Nutrition Information

Calories 312.7 kcal

Carbohydrate 14.2 g
Cholesterol 26.0 mg

A timeless classic; add grilled chicken or shrimp for extra protein.

Ingredients

For the salad For the Caesar dressing
Directions
Make the dressing

Whisk the mayonnaise, anchovies, garlic, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce, and Parmesan until smooth.

Toss the lettuce

Place the chopped romaine in a large bowl and toss with the dressing.

Add crunch

Top with croutons and shaved Parmesan.

Serve

Serve immediately.
