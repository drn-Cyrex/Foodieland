Crispy Tofu Veggie Stir Fry

Crispy tofu tossed with colorful vegetables in a savory sauce.

Mei Ling

6 April 2024
prep time8minutes
cook time12minutes
vegan

Nutrition Information

Calories 289.6 kcal
Total Fat 14.2 g

Carbohydrate 24.3 g
Cholesterol 0 mg

Pressing the tofu before cooking helps it get extra crispy.

Ingredients

For the stir fry
Directions
Crisp the tofu

Toss the tofu cubes in cornstarch and pan-fry in sesame oil until golden on all sides.

Stir-fry the vegetables

Add the pepper, broccoli, carrot, and ginger and stir-fry for 4–5 minutes.

Add sauce

Pour in the soy sauce and toss to coat everything evenly.

Serve

Top with green onions and serve with rice.
