Firecracker Vegan Lettuce Wraps - Spicy!

Directions
Sauté the filling

Heat the sesame oil, sauté the garlic and mushrooms for 2 minutes, then add the lentils.

Make it fiery

Stir in the soy sauce, sriracha, and maple syrup and cook for 2–3 minutes.

Assemble

Spoon the filling into lettuce leaves and top with carrots, cilantro, and sesame seeds.

Serve

Serve immediately as a spicy hand-held snack.
