Classic Grilled Steak

Juicy ribeye steaks seasoned with salt, pepper, rosemary, thyme, and garlic.

Brandon Cole

cook time15minutes

Ingredients

For the steak
Directions
Season the steaks

Rub the steaks with olive oil, salt, pepper, rosemary, and thyme.

Preheat the grill

Preheat a grill or grill pan to high heat.

Grill

Grill the steaks for 4–5 minutes per side for medium-rare, adjusting for thickness and preferred doneness. If using a grill pan, add the smashed garlic to the pan for aroma.

Rest

Remove the steaks and let them rest for 5 minutes before slicing.

Serve

Slice against the grain and serve.
