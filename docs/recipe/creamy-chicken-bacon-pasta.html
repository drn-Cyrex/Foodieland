Creamy Chicken and Bacon Pasta

Fettuccine tossed with tender chicken, smoky bacon, and a rich Parmesan cream sauce.

Oliver Grant

5 March 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time20minutes
meat

Nutrition Information

Carbohydrate 48.2 g
Cholesterol 105.0 mg

Rich and indulgent; a smaller portion with a side salad balances it nicely.

Ingredients

For the pasta For the cream sauce
Directions
Cook the pasta

Boil the fettuccine in salted water until al dente, then drain, reserving a little pasta water.

Crisp the bacon

Cook the chopped bacon in a large pan until crisp, then transfer it to a plate.

Cook the chicken

In the same pan, cook the sliced chicken until golden and cooked through.

Make the sauce

Add the garlic and butter to the pan and cook briefly. Pour in the cream and simmer gently, then stir in the Parmesan, salt, and pepper.

Combine and serve

Toss the pasta, bacon, and chicken with the cream sauce, adding a splash of reserved pasta water if needed. Serve immediately.
