Tomato Basil Soup

Warm tomato soup with fresh basil and a smooth texture.

19 March 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time20minutes
lunch

Vegan and cozy; pairs perfectly with crusty bread.

Ingredients

For main dish
Directions
Sauté the aromatics

Heat the olive oil and sauté the onion and garlic until soft.

Add tomatoes

Add the chopped tomatoes, broth, salt, and pepper, and simmer for 15 minutes.

Blend

Blend the soup until smooth, then return to the pot.

Add basil and serve

Stir in the fresh basil and serve warm.
