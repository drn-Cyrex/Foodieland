Pesto Pasta Bowl

Pasta tossed with fresh basil pesto, cherry tomatoes, and grated Parmesan.

Giulia Moretti

17 April 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time10minutes
lunch

Nutrition Information

Calories 398.6 kcal
Total Fat 18.7 g
Protein 11.4 g
Carbohydrate 47.2 g
Cholesterol 12.0 mg

Fresh pesto makes all the difference; toast the pine nuts for extra flavor.

Ingredients

For the pasta For the pesto
Directions
Cook the pasta

Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente, then drain, reserving a little pasta water.

Make the pesto

Blend the basil, pine nuts, olive oil, garlic, Parmesan, and salt until smooth.

Combine

Toss the pasta with the pesto, adding a splash of pasta water to loosen.

Serve

Top with cherry tomatoes and extra Parmesan and serve.
