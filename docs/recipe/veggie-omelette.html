Veggie Omelette

Fluffy omelette packed with fresh vegetables.

Isabella Reed

17 May 2024
prep time5minutes
cook time7minutes
breakfast

Nutrition Information

Calories 198.3 kcal
Total Fat 13.2 g
Protein 14.1 g
Carbohydrate 5.8 g
Cholesterol 372.0 mg

A vegetable-rich omelette that is low in carbs and high in protein.

Ingredients

For main dish
Directions
Whisk the eggs

Beat the eggs with salt and black pepper until smooth.

Sauté the vegetables

Heat the olive oil in a non-stick pan and sauté the pepper and onion for 2 minutes, then add the spinach and wilt.

Add the eggs

Pour the eggs over the vegetables and cook over medium-low heat until nearly set.

Fold the omelette

Fold the omelette in half and cook for another minute.

Serve

Slide onto a plate and serve immediately.
