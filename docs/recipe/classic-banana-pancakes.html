Classic Banana Pancakes

Soft banana pancakes with a naturally sweet flavor and fluffy texture.

Michael Smith

18 February 2024
prep time10minutes
cook time5minutes
breakfast

Ripe bananas add natural sweetness, so little added sugar is needed.

Ingredients

For main dish For cooking and serving
Directions
Mash the banana

In a large bowl, mash the ripe banana with a fork until smooth.

Combine wet ingredients

Whisk the egg, milk, and vanilla extract into the mashed banana.

Add dry ingredients

Stir in the flour, sugar, and baking powder until just combined; do not overmix.

Cook the pancakes

Heat a buttered pan over medium heat. Pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake, cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden brown.

Serve

Plate the pancakes, top with sliced banana and a drizzle of maple syrup, and serve warm.
